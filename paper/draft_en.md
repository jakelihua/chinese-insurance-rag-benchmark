## Abstract

When cold-starting a domain-specific retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system before user query logs exist, practitioners often synthesize queries from knowledge-base passages to rank candidate retrievers. This paper examines two practical questions in Chinese insurance retrieval: (1) when does ranking retrievers on synthetic queries reverse the winner observed on real user questions, and (2) to what extent do format-valid synthetic rewrites drop or alter underwriting and claims eligibility conditions across model scales?

We study these questions in three rounds. In **Round 1**, we deduplicate $3{,}746$ Chinese insurance answers and partition QA pairs into $3{,}725$ connected components via Union-Find so that no shared answer spans calibration ($750$ queries) and test ($2{,}994$ queries) splits. Evaluating six lexical retrievers, we show that a source-prefix diagnostic proxy selects character $3\text{--}5$-gram TF-IDF ($\text{MRR@10}=0.9330$) over character-bigram BM25 ($0.9311$), whereas real test queries favor bigram BM25 ($0.3817$ vs. $0.2727$, selection regret $0.1090$). In **Round 2**, we add dense (`bge_small_zh`) and hybrid (`rrf_bm25_bge`) retrievers and generate zero-shot and 3-shot synthetic queries from held-out answers using both a local 1.5B model (`Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, $N=249$ common valid sources) and a cloud production model (`Qwen-Plus`, $N=179$). On the full real test set ($N=2{,}994$), pure dense retrieval ranks first ($\text{MRR@10}=0.5216$ vs. $0.5030$ for hybrid RRF; paired difference $+0.0187$, 10,000-resample 95% bootstrap CI $[+0.0077, +0.0296]$; zero-truncation sliding-window `Full-MaxP` over all $509$ long documents [$762$ overflow tail chunks] confirms $0.5165$ vs. $0.5007$, $\Delta = +0.0158$). On synthetic queries from both models and both prompting modes, the point winner reverses to hybrid RRF (full-test regret $0.0187$). Crucially, aligning real test queries with synthetic queries on the exact same target documents ($n=209$ queries across $207$ docs for `1.5B`; $n=153$ queries across $151$ docs for `Qwen-Plus`) shows that on these matched subsamples, real RRF already slightly leads real BGE in point estimate ($0.4975$ vs. $0.4696$ and $0.4980$ vs. $0.4668$, with 95% CIs $[-0.0645, +0.0096]$ and $[-0.0687, +0.0073]$ spanning zero), and while `zero_shot` significantly elevates query-to-source bigram containment $\kappa_2(q', d^*)$ ($0.3253 \rightarrow 0.4775$ on `1.5B` and $0.3167 \rightarrow 0.4638$ on `Qwen-Plus`) and boosts `bm25_char2` by $+0.0925$ ($95\%$ CI $[+0.0227, +0.1633]$) and $+0.2457$ ($[+0.1695, +0.3216]$) MRR@10 on those paired queries, the paired difference-in-differences in RRF-over-BGE advantage ($\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}} = +0.0123$, CI $[-0.0375, +0.0649]$ on `1.5B zero_shot`; $-0.0020$, CI $[-0.0479, +0.0438]$ on `Qwen-Plus zero_shot`) spans zero across all four settings, meaning within-source phrasing effects on the relative RRF$-$BGE gap cannot be statistically separated from source-subsampling variance at current sample sizes. Marginal query-feature importance weighting fails ($0\%$ optimal selection), whereas an exploratory retrospective **Relational Source-Overlap Gate** (`source_overlap_gated`, filtering synthetic queries by $\kappa_2(q', d^*)$) recovers `bge_small_zh` across calibrated budgets $B \in \{25, 50, 100, 200\}$ and cold-start quantiles $q \in [0.10, 0.30]$ (flipping back to RRF for $q \ge 0.40\text{--}0.50$), while paired-bootstrap **Candidate-Set Abstention** on small real calibration sets avoids confident wrong commitments at $B=25$ ($0\%$ confident wrong rate, $80\%$ abstention, $100\%$ optimal coverage, mean set size $2.60$ of $8$). In **Round 3**, we build $80$ controlled clause-grounded cases with double-witness counterexamples from five official insurance policy wordings and evaluate $120$ paired rewrites across `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`, explicitly separating **All-Slot Completeness** (`all_slots_preserved`) from reference-independent **Formal Decision Determinacy** (enumerating complete threshold-partitioned equivalence regions of missing slots without reading ground-truth `seed_frame` metadata). All $120/120$ rewrites pass permissive `format_valid` checks ($33/40$ on `1.5B` and $40/40$ on `Qwen-Plus`/`Qwen-Max` under strict single-`?`), yet unconstrained conversational rewriting (`ordinary_style`) preserves standalone all-slot completeness in only $30\%$, $0\%$, and $20\%$ of seeds (and standalone decision determinacy in $30\%$, $0\%$, and $45\%$), whereas explicit slot constraints (`condition_prompt`) raise all-slot match to $95\%\text{--}100\%$ and decision determinacy to $95\%\text{--}100\%$. Finally, a $64$-case **synthetic multi-threshold rule-compiler stress test** inspired by healthcare clinical pathways and labor-law provisions ($48$ method records from $42$ local generations) shows that pairing `condition_prompt` with a 1-step closed-loop **slot-guard repair** (`slot_guard_repair`) lifts parser-verified all-slot match from $6.2\%$ (`ordinary_style`) and $37.5\%$ (`condition_prompt`) to $68.8\%$ ($11/16$), raises reference-independent decision determinacy to $75.0\%$ ($12/16$), and eliminates $100\%$ of parser-detected threshold mutations ($31.2\% \rightarrow 0.0\%$).

## 1. Introduction

Selecting a retriever for a vertical-domain RAG system—for instance, choosing among character $n$-gram BM25, dense bi-encoders, or Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)—often happens before real user traffic is available. A common workflow in both retrieval research and automated RAG evaluation frameworks is to generate synthetic questions from corpus passages using an instruction-tuned LLM, score candidate retrievers on the synthetic set, and deploy the top-ranked system.

While prior work has studied macro-level rank correlations between synthetic and real test collections, two specific failure modes matter when making concrete deployment decisions in insurance retrieval:
1. **Dense vs. Hybrid Selection Reversal (Source-Sampling Confounding + Lexical Copy Inflation)**: Comparing a small synthetic sample ($179\text{--}249$ passages) against a full deployment test distribution ($2{,}994$ queries) simultaneously alters the target document subsample and the query phrasing style. When passage-conditioned query generation reuses domain terms from the source passage, BM25 scores are significantly elevated on those sources, while paired difference-in-differences tests show that at $n=209/153$, the change in the relative RRF$-$BGE gap has 95% CIs spanning zero and cannot be statistically separated from document-sampling variance.
2. **Loss of Underwriting and Claims Conditions**: In insurance clauses, coverage outcomes (`WAIT_EXCLUDED` vs. `WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED`) depend on specific variables: product identity, first-time vs. renewal enrollment, policy effective day, sickness vs. accidental injury cause, plan-specific waiting period (e.g., 7 vs. 25 days), and insurer waiver approval. A rewrite that satisfies standard format filters (single line, question mark, valid length) can still drop or alter one of these variables, making the question undecidable (`CLARIFY`) or flipping its coverage outcome. Crucially, omitting a required slot does not always make the rule outcome indeterminate (e.g., if a claim occurs after a 30-day waiting period has already elapsed, omitting whether the cause was sickness or accident leaves both admissible completions at `WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED`), requiring evaluators to separate **all-slot completeness** from **reference-independent formal decision determinacy**.

This paper reports a three-round empirical and methodological study on Chinese insurance retrieval, pairing diagnostic failure analysis with concrete, falsifiable mitigations. To avoid terminology ambiguity, we explicitly distinguish two levels of abstention studied in this work:
- **Retriever-Selection Candidate-Set Abstention (Round 2)**: At the benchmark evaluation level, returning a bootstrap confidence set of candidate retrievers $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{0.95}$ rather than committing to a single noisy winner when the real calibration budget $B$ cannot statistically separate top contenders.
- **Query-Level Clarification Abstention (`CLARIFY`, Round 3)**: At the individual question level, withholding a binary exclusion decision when a pivotal underwriting condition slot is omitted, formally certified via two opposite-outcome completion witnesses.

Our scope is intentionally bounded: we evaluate retrieval selection and clause condition conservation rather than end-to-end online claims adjudication, and we separate deterministic slot checks from human verification. Specifically:
- **Round 1 (Group-Disjoint Lexical Baseline)**: On $3{,}746$ deduplicated answers from a public Chinese insurance QA corpus partitioned into $3{,}725$ connected groups ($750$ calibration, $2{,}994$ held-out test queries), we compare six lexical retrievers against extractive keyword and prefix-copy proxies, quantifying how long-$n$-gram copy bias selects `tfidf_char3_5` over the true lexical winner `bm25_char2` (regret $0.1090$ MRR@10).
- **Round 2 (Source-Matched Decomposition, Source-Overlap Gating, and Candidate-Set Abstention)**: Expanding to eight systems (six lexical, `bge_small_zh`, and `rrf_bm25_bge`), we evaluate zero-shot and 3-shot queries generated by local `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` ($N=249$ common valid sources) and cloud `Qwen-Plus` ($N=179$). Both models select `rrf_bm25_bge` under both prompting strategies, reversing the full real-test winner `bge_small_zh` ($\text{MRR@10}=0.5216$ vs. $0.5030$; confirmed by a zero-truncation sliding-window `Full-MaxP` ablation over $762$ overflow tail chunks at $0.5165$ vs. $0.5007$, $\Delta = +0.0158$). By aligning real test queries with synthetic queries on the exact same target documents ($n=209$ for `1.5B`, $n=153$ for `Qwen-Plus`), we quantify both source-sampling shift and within-source lexical copy inflation ($\kappa_2(q', d^*)$ and paired difference-in-differences $\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}}$). We show why marginal query-feature density weighting fails ($0\%$ optimal selection), evaluate an exploratory retrospective **relational source-overlap gate** (`source_overlap_gated`) across calibrated budgets $B \in \{25, 50, 100, 200\}$ and a full cold-start quantile sweep $q \in [0.10, 1.00]$ against matched-size random/length baselines, and demonstrate that paired-bootstrap **candidate-set abstention** on small real calibration samples reduces confident wrong retriever commitments at $B=25$ from $60\%\text{--}100\%$ to $0\%$.
- **Round 3 (Double-Witness Counterexamples, Slot Completeness vs. Decision Determinacy, and Synthetic Rule-Compiler Stress Test)**: Using five official Chinese insurance policy clauses, we formalize a waiting-period rule compiler and construct $20$ seed scenarios ($80$ controlled variants where every `missing_critical` `CLARIFY` case carries two opposite-outcome completion witnesses) alongside $120$ paired LLM rewrites across `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`. We separate numeric value mutations ($15\%$ on `1.5B` vs. $0/20$ observed on `Qwen-Plus`/`Qwen-Max` in this 20-seed sample) from persistent conversational slot omission across the tested models, and show that explicit slot constraints (`condition_prompt`) raise standalone all-slot match from $0\%\text{--}30\%$ to $95\%\text{--}100\%$ and standalone decision determinacy from $0\%\text{--}45\%$ to $95\%\text{--}100\%$. Extending the same compiler and double-witness protocol to a $16$-seed ($64$ controlled cases, $48$ method records from $42$ LLM calls) **synthetic multi-threshold rule-compiler stress test** inspired by healthcare clinical eligibility and labor-law compliance, we further show that pairing `condition_prompt` with a 1-step deterministic **slot-guard repair** loop (`slot_guard_repair`) raises parser-verified all-slot completeness from $6.2\%$ (`ordinary_style`) and $37.5\%$ (`condition_prompt`) to $68.8\%$ ($11/16$), lifts reference-independent formal decision determinacy to $75.0\%$ ($12/16$), and eliminates $100\%$ of parser-detected threshold-overwriting mutations ($31.2\% \rightarrow 0.0\%$).

## 2. Related Work

- **Synthetic Queries in IR Evaluation**: Promptagator and InPars-v2 use LLM-generated queries to train retrievers when in-domain labels are scarce. Evaluation frameworks such as ARES, RAGAs, and RAGEval (Zhu et al., 2025) also use synthetic queries to score RAG pipelines. Recent analyses by Rahmani et al. (2024) and van Elburg et al. (2025) note that LLM-generated evaluation sets carry generator-specific biases and diverge from authentic user queries. We decompose synthetic-vs-real retriever ranking differences into document-sampling variance and passage-level lexical copy inflation ($\kappa_2(q', d^*)$) across both 1.5B and commercial cloud generators.
- **Calibration and Abstention under Small Budgets**: Prediction-Powered Inference (PPI) and ARES combine synthetic predictions with small gold samples to construct confidence intervals on paired data. In cold-start retriever selection, however, synthetic queries are generated from documents without paired real user questions on the same test split. We evaluate unpaired shrinkage and surface-density weighting as negative baselines, and formulate a bootstrap candidate-set abstention rule that withholds point commitment when calibration uncertainty spans multiple systems.
- **Insurance QA Benchmarks**: InsuranceQA and its Chinese adaptations provide community question-answering pairs for answer selection. Because community answers contain duplicates and lack formal underwriting parameters, we augment our Round 1–2 FAQ experiments with a Round 3 benchmark built directly from five official Chinese insurance policy wordings with deterministic waiting-period rules.

## 3. Round 1: Group-Disjoint Split and Lexical Proxy Diagnostics

### 3.1 Leakage-Free Group Split
Public Chinese insurance FAQ corpora (`baoxianzhidao_filter.csv`, $8{,}362$ raw rows) contain repeated questions and shared boilerplate answers. Filtering for `is_best == '1'`, length bounds ($4 \le |q| \le 500$, $15 \le |d| \le 3{,}000$), and PII removal yields $3{,}746$ unique answers and $3{,}744$ valid QA groups. We build an undirected graph linking any pairs that share normalized questions, normalized answers, or character $2\text{--}4$-gram TF-IDF title cosine similarity $\ge 0.90$, yielding $3{,}725$ connected components via Union-Find. Assigning whole components to splits (`seed=20261025`) produces:
- **Real Calibration Pool**: $750$ queries across $745$ components (zero shared answers with test; max cross-split title cosine $0.8965$).
- **Real Test Set**: $2{,}994$ queries across $2{,}980$ components.

### 3.2 Lexical Systems and Round 1 Results

### 4.2 Decomposing Selection Reversal: Source-Sampling Confounding vs. Lexical Copy Inflation
1. **Full-Test Ordering & Full-Document MaxP Sensitivity Check**: On the $2{,}994$ held-out real questions, `bge_small_zh` achieves $\text{MRR@10}=0.5216$ ($\text{Hit@5}=0.6453$), beating `rrf_bm25_bge` ($0.5030$, $\text{Hit@5}=0.6075$) by $+0.0187$ (10,000-resample paired bootstrap 95% CI: $[+0.0077, +0.0296]$). To rule out 512-token truncation on the $509$ long documents as a confounder, our standalone reproducible `Full-MaxP` sliding-window ablation (`src/maxp_ablation.py`, `results/validation_v2/mitigation_and_maxp_diagnostics.json`, $512$-token windows with $256$-token stride, $762$ overflow tail chunks across $1{,}271$ total windows, $0$ truncated answers; retokenizing each decoded tail string confirms $\le 510$ content tokens and $536/762$ exact token-ID matches) shows `bge_small_zh` still significantly outperforms `rrf_bm25_bge` on `real_test` ($\text{MRR@10}=0.5165$ vs. $0.5007$, $\Delta = +0.0158$, 95% CI $[+0.0049, +0.0267]$; $\text{Hit@5}=0.6389$ vs. $0.6055$).
2. **Disentangling Source-Sampling Confounding from Within-Source Lexical Copy Inflation (`\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}}`)**: Because comparing $N=2{,}994$ real test queries against $N=249$ (`1.5B`) or $N=179$ (`Qwen-Plus`) synthetic queries changes both the target document subset and the query generator, we align real test queries with synthetic queries on the exact same target documents ($207$ matched test documents / $n=209$ real test queries for `1.5B`; $151$ matched test documents / $n=153$ real test queries for `Qwen-Plus`). On these source-matched real subsets, `bm25_char2` happens to be slightly stronger ($0.4073$ and $0.3909$ vs. $0.3817$ full-test) and `bge_small_zh` slightly weaker ($0.4696$ and $0.4668$ vs. $0.5216$ full-test), so `rrf_bm25_bge` already holds a small, statistically non-significant point lead on the matched real queries ($0.4975$ vs. $0.4696$, $\Delta_{\text{BGE}-\text{RRF}} = -0.0279$, 95% CI $[-0.0645, +0.0096]$ for `1.5B`; $0.4980$ vs. $0.4668$, $\Delta_{\text{BGE}-\text{RRF}} = -0.0312$, 95% CI $[-0.0687, +0.0073]$ for `Qwen-Plus`). Thus, **subsampling only $179\text{--}249$ passages introduces substantial document-sampling variance that confounds raw point-winner comparisons against the full $2{,}994$-query deployment distribution**. Holding the $209$ (`1.5B`) and $153$ (`Qwen-Plus`) query-aligned pairs fixed, `zero_shot` synthesis increases mean query-to-source bigram containment $\kappa_2(q, d^*)$ from $0.3253$ to $0.4775$ ($0.4789$ on the $207$ unique docs) on `1.5B` and from $0.3167$ to $0.4638$ ($0.4651$ on $151$ unique docs) on `Qwen-Plus`, significantly inflating `bm25_char2` MRR@10 from $0.4073 \rightarrow 0.4998$ ($0.5036$ on $207$ unique docs; paired gain $+0.0925$, 95% CI $[+0.0227, +0.1633]$) on `1.5B` and from $0.3909 \rightarrow 0.6366$ ($0.6450$ on $151$ unique docs; paired gain $+0.2457$, 95% CI $[+0.1695, +0.3216]$) on `Qwen-Plus`. However, when we test the paired difference-in-differences in RRF's advantage over BGE, $\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}} = (\text{MRR}_{\text{RRF}} - \text{MRR}_{\text{BGE}})_{\text{syn}} - (\text{MRR}_{\text{RRF}} - \text{MRR}_{\text{BGE}})_{\text{real}}$, all four 10,000-resample paired bootstrap 95% CIs span zero under both query-equal-weighted resampling and source-document-clustered resampling (which averages per unique target document before resampling documents with equal weight): query-weighted $+0.0123$ (CI $[-0.0375, +0.0649]$; document-equal-weighted mean $+0.0185$, clustered CI $[-0.0314, +0.0698]$) on `1.5B zero_shot`, query-weighted $-0.0259$ (CI $[-0.0736, +0.0217]$; doc-weighted mean $-0.0225$, clustered CI $[-0.0710, +0.0258]$) on `1.5B few_shot_3`, query-weighted $-0.0020$ (CI $[-0.0479, +0.0438]$; doc-weighted mean $-0.0036$, clustered CI $[-0.0474, +0.0421]$) on `Qwen-Plus zero_shot`, and query-weighted $-0.0298$ (CI $[-0.0791, +0.0204]$; doc-weighted mean $-0.0320$, clustered CI $[-0.0813, +0.0187]$) on `Qwen-Plus few_shot_3`. Thus, while `zero_shot` demonstrably inflates $\kappa_2$ and `bm25_char2`, at $n=209/153$ we cannot claim that within-source phrasing statistically significantly widens the RRF$-$BGE gap beyond source-sampling shift. Providing 3 calibration examples (`few_shot_3`) reduces $\kappa_2$ to $0.3180$ (`1.5B`) and $0.4108$ (`Qwen-Plus`), shrinking the uncalibrated RRF-over-BGE difference on $N=249/179$ to $+0.0100$ (95% CI $[-0.0208, +0.0414]$) and $+0.0019$ (95% CI $[-0.0394, +0.0428]$).
3. **Why Marginal Reweighting Fails vs. Exploratory Relational Source-Overlap Gating (`source_overlap_gated`)**: A 2-fold cross-fit character $1\text{--}3$-gram logistic classifier separates real calibration from synthetic queries with $\text{ROC-AUC}=0.8120$ (`zero_shot`) and $0.7733$ (`few_shot_3`) at $B=25$ (rising to $0.8765$ and $0.9148$ at $B=200$), yet `synthetic_density_weighted` selects `bge_small_zh` in **$0\%$ of trials** across all budgets because the relative advantage between pure dense and hybrid retrieval is governed by the relational query-to-source bigram containment $\kappa_2(q, d^*) = |\text{Bi}(q) \cap \text{Bi}(d^*)| / |\text{Bi}(q)|$. Within the low-overlap stratum ($\kappa_2 < 0.25$, $40.0\%$ of `real_test`, $n=1{,}199$), `bge_small_zh` beats `rrf_bm25_bge` on `real_test` ($0.3022$ vs. $0.2220$, $\Delta = +0.0801$) **and on all four LLM synthetic sets** (`1.5B zero_shot` $+0.0839$, `1.5B few_shot_3` $+0.0326$, `Qwen-Plus zero_shot` $+0.0821$, `Qwen-Plus few_shot_3` $+0.1600$), whereas `rrf_bm25_bge` dominates in the high-overlap stratum ($\kappa_2 \ge 0.50$: $-0.0457$ on `real_test`, $[-0.0811, -0.0447]$ on synthetic sets). Filtering synthetic queries by the calibrated real-query median $\kappa_2(q', d^*) \le \hat{\tau}_{\text{cal}}^{(B)}$—an **exploratory retrospective diagnostic (`source_overlap_gated`)**—selects `bge_small_zh` across all 5 calibration draws for $B \in \{25, 50, 100, 200\}$ on both LLM scales ($0.0000$ full-test regret). At cold-start $B=0$ (deterministic filtering on a fixed synthetic set rather than independent trials), a full quantile sensitivity sweep ($q \in \{0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70, 0.80, 1.00\}$) shows that `source_overlap_gated` selects `bge_small_zh` across all four synthetic sets for $q \in [0.10, 0.30]$ (and up to $q=0.40$ on `1.5B zero_shot`, $q=0.60$ on `1.5B few_shot_3`, and $q=0.80$ on `Qwen-Plus few_shot_3`), before flipping back to `rrf_bm25_bge` once high-overlap queries enter at $q \ge 0.40\text{--}0.50$. ### 5.3 Key Findings on Condition Drift
1. **Separating Slot Completeness from Reference-Independent Decision Determinacy**: In `src/analyze_v3.py`, we explicitly separate **All-Slot Completeness** (`surface_v2_preserved`, requiring every required slot in $z$ to be recovered) from **Reference-Independent Formal Decision Determinacy** (`enumerate_admissible_decisions` and `evaluate_rule_outcome_under_observed`, which enumerate complete threshold-equivalence regions $\{5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120\}$ across all waiting-period thresholds $\{7, 15, 25, 30, 90\}$ using only extracted question observations and public policy rules—never reading ground-truth `seed_frame` metadata—and check reachability and slot mutations across all six schema fields `ALL_INSURANCE_FIELDS`, including optional waiver slots not in a seed's minimal `required_fields`: returning `PRESERVED_DECISION` iff no schema field is mutated and the reachable set is the singleton $[f(z)]$, `COUNTERFACTUAL_FLIP` / `CONDITION_MUTATED` if any schema field is mutated or the unique reachable decision flips, and `DEGRADED_TO_CLARIFY` when $|\text{reachable}| \neq 1$). For example, when a claim occurs on Day 45 after a 30-day waiting period (`pa_h865`), omitting `cause` (sickness vs. accident) fails all-slot completeness but still yields `WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED` under every admissible completion. Thus, under `ordinary_style` on `Qwen-Max`, standalone all-slot match is $20\%$ ($4/20$) while standalone decision determinacy is $45\%$ ($9/20$); on `Qwen-Plus` `condition_prompt`, all-slot match is $95\%$ ($19/20$, due to one omitted non-pivotal `cause` on a renewal-waiver seed) while decision determinacy is **$100\%$ ($20/20$)**.
2. **Format Validation Does Not Catch Condition Loss**: All $120/120$ generated questions pass permissive `format_valid` ($33/40$ on `1.5B` and $40/40$ on `Qwen-Plus`/`Qwen-Max` under strict single-`?`), yet under `ordinary_style`, $70\%$ (`1.5B`), $100\%$ (`Qwen-Plus`), and $55\%$ (`Qwen-Max`) fail to preserve standalone rule decidability.
3. **Value Mutation on `1.5B` vs. Persistent Conversational Omission Across Tested Models**: On `1.5B`, `ordinary_style` mutates `"保单生效第20天"` into `"保单生效后的第二天"` in $3/20$ seeds (`condition-013, 015, 016`) and collapses `condition-009` (`WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED`, 7-day wait) and `condition-010` (`WAIT_EXCLUDED`, 25-day wait) into the exact same unanswerable string. Moving to the commercial API models `Qwen-Plus` and `Qwen-Max` (undisclosed parameter counts) reduces observed numeric mutations on this 20-seed sample to $0\%$ ($0/20$; as a finite sample observation, this does not prove zero population error), but both models still omit product names ($100\%$ on `Qwen-Plus`, $50\%$ on `Qwen-Max`) or rewrite first-time enrollment into ambiguous phrasing ($30\%$ on `Qwen-Max`) when instructed to sound colloquial.
4. **Slot-Constrained Prompting (`condition_prompt`) Across Tested Models**: Explicitly constraining required slots restores standalone all-slot completeness to $95\%$ (`1.5B` and `Qwen-Plus`) and $100\%$ (`Qwen-Max`), achieves $95\%\text{--}100\%$ standalone decision determinacy ($100\%$ granted-policy determinacy across all three models), and lifts 5-clause `bm25_char2` Top-1 accuracy to $95\%\text{--}100\%$.

## 6. Synthetic Multi-Threshold Rule-Compiler Stress Test (Inspired by Healthcare and Labor-Law Rules)

To test whether condition drop, numeric threshold mutation, and closed-loop repair operate on other multi-threshold decision structures beyond insurance waiting periods, we construct a **synthetic rule-compiler stress test** (`src/cross_domain_study.py`, `data_v3/cross_domain_controlled_cases.jsonl`, `data_v3/cross_domain_summary.json`). **Provenance Note**: Unlike the five verbatim official insurance PDFs in Section 5, these four passages are *researcher-constructed semi-synthetic rule texts* inspired by clinical pathways and Chinese labor statutes (standardizing continuous day thresholds such as $14$, $30$, $90$, and $365$ days and explicit waiver clauses for deterministic rule compilation; they are **not** verbatim clinical guidelines or statutory advice). The stress test spans $16$ seed scenarios, $64$ controlled cases with verified double-witness counterexamples, and $48$ method records reconstructed from $42$ local `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` `float32/eager` calls via the `repaired` flags in `cross_domain_generations.jsonl` ($16$ `ordinary_style` + $16$ `condition_prompt` + $10$ targeted repair calls, reusing the $6$ already-complete `condition_prompt` outputs):
- **Healthcare-Inspired Pathway Rules (`healthcare`, 2 synthetic clauses, 8 seeds, 32 cases)**:
  1. `med_egfr_tki` (*Synthetic Osimertinib Outpatient Special-Drug Protocol*): models a 14-day washout threshold after prior TKI therapy for second-line conversion, with first-line or specialist emergency waiver exemptions.
  2. `med_vte_prophylaxis` (*Synthetic Post-Op LMWH VTE Prophylaxis Pathway*): models a 10-day window for knee replacement vs. 35-day window for hip replacement, with active bleeding contraindicated unless a hematology waiver applies.
- **Labor-Law-Inspired Compliance Rules (`legal`, 2 synthetic clauses, 8 seeds, 32 cases)**:
  3. `law_work_injury` (*Synthetic Commuting Traffic Accident Filing Rule*): models non-principal traffic fault and a 30-day employer vs. 365-day employee/kin filing window, with written HRSS extension exemptions.
  4. `law_noncompete` (*Synthetic Non-Compete Termination Rule*): models confidentiality-bound personnel and a $>90$-day employer non-payment threshold, with grace-period agreement exemptions.

We evaluate three generation regimes on `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` using a hardened slot parser (`extract_cross_slots_v2`) and reference-independent decision enumerator (`enumerate_cross_decisions`) that expand unobserved duration slots across a complete threshold-equivalence partition ($\{7, 10, 12, 14, 20, 30, 32, 35, 45, 90, 120, 365, 400\}$, covering $< t, = t, > t$ across all rule thresholds $\{10, 14, 30, 35, 90, 365\}$), enforce negation/discontinuation precedence (`"不负有保密义务"` $\to$ `disqualified`, `"已停用常规抗凝"` $\to$ `secondary`, `"尚未获…特批"` / `"未批准延长申请时限"` $\to$ `approved_waiver=False`, explicit uncertainty `"尚不清楚"` $\to$ `unknown`), and never access ground-truth `seed_frame` metadata during reachability enumeration. Because both repair triggering and evaluation use `extract_cross_slots_v2`, these gains measure consistency under our audited rule compiler and slot parser rather than independent human-judged semantic accuracy.

## 7. Practical Recommendations and Limitations

- **Practical Recommendations**:
  1. **Control for source-sampling variance and audit query-to-source lexical overlap ($\kappa_2(q', d^*)$)**: Always evaluate synthetic queries on matched target document subsets alongside the full deployment collection, and inspect sensitivity across overlap quantiles ($q \in [0.10, 0.30]$) rather than trusting raw synthetic point winners.
  2. **Use small group-disjoint real calibration sets with candidate-set abstention**: When evaluating directly on $25\text{--}50$ real queries, paired-bootstrap candidate-set abstention avoids premature point commitment while retaining the true winner in $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{0.95}$.
  3. **Separate all-slot completeness from formal decision determinacy and pair `condition_prompt` with closed-loop slot repair**: Enumerate admissible completions to distinguish benign slot omissions from genuine ambiguity (`CLARIFY`), and use `slot_guard_repair` to catch threshold-overwriting mutations.
- **Limitations**: First, our Round 1–2 FAQ corpus comes from a single community insurance dataset with weak historical answer labels; while our `Full-MaxP` sliding-window ablation ($762$ overflow tail chunks across $509$ long answers, $0$ truncated answers) confirms that `bge_small_zh` still outperforms `rrf_bm25_bge` on `real_test` ($+0.0158$ MRR@10), and our source-matched analysis ($n=209$ and $n=153$) explicitly quantifies document-sampling confounding, prospective confirmation on a larger source sample ($N \ge 1{,}000$) is needed to narrow matched-subset confidence intervals. Second, `source_overlap_gated` was developed retrospectively on the observed Round 2 datasets and is sensitive to the cutoff quantile ($q \ge 0.40\text{--}0.50$ reverts to RRF). Third, our experiments evaluate models within the Qwen family (`Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`), our Section 6 benchmark uses researcher-constructed synthetic rule texts rather than verbatim statutes, and independent two-rater human annotation (`reviewer_A.html` and `reviewer_B.html`, scored via `score_annotations.py`) remains pending.
