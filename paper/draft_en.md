## Abstract

When cold-starting or benchmarking a domain-specific retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system, practitioners routinely synthesize evaluation queries from source documents using instruction-tuned LLMs. While recent work has shown that synthetic queries can shift macro-level retriever rankings, a critical failure mode in rule-governed vertical domains remains unverified: *a synthetic query can achieve $100\%$ Top-1 retrieval while silently dropping or mutating pivotal eligibility conditions, either flipping the ground-truth business decision or collapsing a determinate scenario into an ambiguous query that requires user clarification*. This paper introduces a **clause-grounded, decision-preserving verification framework** for synthetic RAG evaluation in Chinese insurance, explicitly separating *all-slot completeness* from reference-independent *formal decision determinacy* (`PRESERVED_DECISION`, `CONDITION_MUTATED`/`COUNTERFACTUAL_FLIP`, and double-witness-certified `DEGRADED_TO_CLARIFY`), preceded by a two-round retrieval-ranking and source-sampling diagnostic baseline. No LLM or embedding model weights are pre-trained or fine-tuned in our study; all experiments evaluate inference-time generation, dense/lexical indexing, statistical calibration, and symbolic rule verification.

In **Rounds 1–2 (Retrieval Ranking & Source-Sampling Diagnostics)**, we deduplicate $3{,}746$ answers from a public historical community QA corpus (`baoxianzhidao_filter.csv`) into $3{,}725$ leakage-free connected components via Union-Find ($750$ calibration, $2{,}994$ held-out test queries). Evaluating six lexical retrievers in Round 1 shows that source-prefix copying selects character $3\text{--}5$-gram TF-IDF ($\text{MRR@10}=0.9330$) over character-bigram BM25 ($0.9311$), whereas real test queries favor bigram BM25 ($0.3817$ vs. $0.2727$, selection regret $0.1090$). In Round 2, we add dense (`bge_small_zh`) and hybrid (`rrf_bm25_bge`) retrievers and generate zero-shot and 3-shot synthetic queries using local `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` ($N=249$ common valid sources) and cloud `Qwen-Plus` ($N=179$). On the full real test set ($N=2{,}994$), pure dense retrieval ranks first ($\text{MRR@10}=0.5216$ vs. $0.5030$ for hybrid RRF; paired difference $+0.0187$, 10,000-resample 95% bootstrap CI $[+0.0077, +0.0296]$; zero-truncation sliding-window `Full-MaxP` over all $509$ long documents [$762$ overflow tail chunks] confirms $0.5165$ vs. $0.5007$, $\Delta = +0.0158$). On synthetic queries from both models and both prompting modes, the point winner reverses to hybrid RRF. Aligning real test queries with synthetic queries on the exact same target documents ($n=209$ queries across $207$ docs for `1.5B`; $n=153$ queries across $151$ docs for `Qwen-Plus`) shows that on these matched subsamples, real RRF already slightly leads real BGE in point estimate ($0.4975$ vs. $0.4696$ and $0.4980$ vs. $0.4668$, with 95% CIs $[-0.0645, +0.0096]$ and $[-0.0687, +0.0073]$ spanning zero), and while `zero_shot` significantly elevates query-to-source bigram containment $\kappa_2(q', d^*)$ ($0.3253 \rightarrow 0.4775$ on `1.5B` and $0.3167 \rightarrow 0.4638$ on `Qwen-Plus`) and boosts `bm25_char2` by $+0.0925$ ($95\%$ CI $[+0.0227, +0.1633]$) and $+0.2457$ ($[+0.1695, +0.3216]$) MRR@10 on those paired queries, the paired difference-in-differences in RRF-over-BGE advantage ($\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}} = +0.0123$, CI $[-0.0375, +0.0649]$ on `1.5B zero_shot`; $-0.0020$, CI $[-0.0479, +0.0438]$ on `Qwen-Plus zero_shot`) spans zero across all four settings, meaning within-source phrasing effects on the relative RRF$-$BGE gap cannot be statistically separated from source-subsampling variance at current sample sizes. We then evaluate an exploratory post-hoc **Relational Source-Overlap Gate** (`source_overlap_gated`, $q \in [0.10, 0.30]$) and paired-bootstrap **Candidate-Set Abstention** across budgets $B \in \{25, 50, 100, 200\}$.

In **Round 3 and Out-of-Sample Confirmation (Clause-Grounded Decision Verification)**, we formalize waiting-period rule compilers over **nine official Chinese insurance policy PDFs**: five development clauses ($20$ seeds, $80$ controlled cases with double-witness counterexamples, $120$ paired rewrites across `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`) and **four newly collected held-out official clauses** ($16$ seeds, $64$ controlled cases, evaluated with the **strictly frozen** Round 3 parser and verifier with zero regex edits). Across the five development clauses, unconstrained conversational rewriting (`ordinary_style`) yields only $30\%$, $0\%$, and $45\%$ standalone decision determinacy across `1.5B`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`, whereas explicit slot constraints (`condition_prompt`) reach $95\%\text{--}100\%$. On the four unseen held-out clauses, the frozen verifier certifies $100\%$ ($64/64$) of controlled cases and shows that `ordinary_style` achieves high template novelty ($1-\kappa_2 = 0.6410$) but $0\%$ ($0/16$) standalone decision determinacy ($31.2\%$ [$5/16$] when granted the source policy title), whereas `condition_prompt` and `slot_guard_repair` reach $93.8\%$ ($15/16$ automatic frozen-parser match; $16/16$ under human semantic audit) with $0\%$ condition mutations. Masking policy titles and insurer names drops 9-clause Top-1 retrieval from $75.0\%$ to $31.2\%\text{--}56.2\%$ (`bm25_char2`) and from $62.5\%\text{--}81.2\%$ to $25.0\%\text{--}56.2\%$ (`bge_small_zh`), proving that standard retrieval scores rely heavily on title anchors while remaining blind to eligibility condition drift. Finally, in an **equal-call-budget comparison** on a $16$-seed ($64$-case) **synthetic multi-threshold rule-compiler stress test**, all non-JSON rewrites achieve $100\%$ Top-1 retrieval on both `bm25_char2` and `bge_small_zh`, yet single-pass JSON rewriting fails format validation ($0/16$ valid, $31.2\%$ decision preservation), and an equal-budget LLM self-check without parser feedback (`generic_self_refine`, $26$ calls) reaches only $37.5\%$ ($6/16$) all-slot match and $50.0\%$ ($8/16$) decision determinacy with $25.0\%$ ($4/16$) threshold mutations (unchanged at $32$ calls). At the exact same $26$-call budget, symbolic parser-guided **`slot_guard_repair`** lifts all-slot match to $68.8\%$ ($11/16$), lifts decision determinacy to $75.0\%$ ($12/16$), and eliminates parser-detected threshold mutations to $0.0\%$ ($0/16$).

## 1. Introduction

Selecting a retriever or curating a synthetic evaluation benchmark for a vertical-domain RAG system—for instance, in insurance underwriting and claims advisory—often happens before real user traffic is available. A standard workflow in both retrieval research and automated RAG evaluation frameworks is to prompt an instruction-tuned LLM to synthesize user questions from corpus passages, filter outputs by surface format rules, and use the resulting synthetic queries to rank retrievers or score end-to-end RAG pipelines.

While recent studies have shown that synthetic queries can alter macro-level retriever rankings relative to real user traffic (Rahmani et al., 2024; van Elburg et al., 2025; Kucia & Gawlik, 2026), a deeper structural flaw threatens synthetic evaluation in rule-governed domains such as insurance: **retrievers score topical and lexical similarity, whereas domain validity depends on discrete eligibility conditions**. In Chinese insurance clauses, whether a claim is excluded by a waiting period (`WAIT_EXCLUDED` vs. `WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED`) is a deterministic function of specific variables: product identity, first-time vs. renewal enrollment, policy effective day, sickness vs. accidental injury cause, plan-specific waiting period (e.g., $7/15/25$ or $30/90$ days), and insurer waiver approval. When an LLM rewrites a seed scenario into a "natural" user question, two distinct failures occur even when the rewrite achieves $100\%$ Top-1 retrieval against the source clause:
1. **Silent Condition Omission and Ambiguity Degradation (`DEGRADED_TO_CLARIFY`)**: Conversational rewrites routinely omit pivotal underwriting slots (reducing query length and raising surface "naturalness"), collapsing a uniquely decidable test case into an underspecified question where multiple opposite coverage outcomes are reachable. Conversely, omitting a *non-pivotal* slot (such as sickness vs. accident cause after the waiting period has already expired) leaves the formal coverage decision uniquely determined. Naive slot-matching scripts conflate these two regimes, whereas standard RAG metrics ignore both.
2. **Condition Mutation and Threshold Overwriting (`CONDITION_MUTATED` / `COUNTERFACTUAL_FLIP`)**: Small local models and open-loop prompts frequently mutate numeric durations (e.g., rewriting "Day 20" into "Day 2") or overwrite a user's actual elapsed days ($7$, $45$, or $120$ days) with the statutory threshold stated in the source clause ($14$ or $90$ days), flipping the ground-truth coverage decision while increasing lexical overlap with the target document.

At the same time, at the corpus retrieval level, comparing candidate retrievers on uncalibrated synthetic query sets conflates *source-subsampling variance* with *within-source lexical copy inflation* ($\kappa_2(q, d^*)$).

To avoid terminology ambiguity, we explicitly distinguish two levels of abstention studied in this work:
- **Retriever-Selection Candidate-Set Abstention (Round 2)**: At the benchmark evaluation level, returning a bootstrap confidence set of candidate retrievers $\widehat{\mathcal{C}}_{0.95}$ rather than committing to a single noisy winner when the real calibration budget $B$ cannot statistically separate top contenders.
- **Query-Level Clarification Abstention (`CLARIFY`, Round 3)**: At the individual question level, withholding a binary exclusion decision when a pivotal underwriting condition slot is omitted, formally certified via two opposite-outcome completion witnesses.

Specifically, our contributions span four stages:
- **Round 1–2 Retrieval Diagnostics (Source-Matched Decomposition, Overlap Gating, and Candidate-Set Abstention)**: On $3{,}746$ deduplicated answers from `baoxianzhidao_filter.csv` partitioned into $3{,}725$ connected groups ($750$ calibration, $2{,}994$ held-out test queries), we compare six lexical retrievers (Round 1) and expand to eight systems (`bge_small_zh` and `rrf_bm25_bge`) across local `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct` ($N=249$) and cloud `Qwen-Plus` ($N=179$) synthetic sets (Round 2), including `Full-MaxP` zero-truncation verification, source-matched difference-in-differences ($\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}}$), exploratory post-hoc `source_overlap_gated`, and paired-bootstrap candidate-set abstention.
- **Round 3 Clause-Grounded Decision Verification Across Model Scales**: Using five official Chinese insurance policy clauses (SHA256-verified with PDF bounding boxes), we compile a formal waiting-period rule verifier to construct $20$ seed scenarios ($80$ controlled variants with double-completion counterexample witnesses) and evaluate $120$ paired rewrites across `Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`, separating strict slot completeness from reference-independent decision determinacy.
- **Out-of-Sample Confirmation on Four Unseen Official Insurance Policies and Title-Masked Retrieval**: On four newly collected official insurance policy PDFs (`pa_2011_36`, `pa_p0254`, `pa_p0321`, `cpic_93896`; $16$ seeds, $64$ controlled cases), we evaluate the **strictly frozen Round 3 slot parser and reachability verifier** (zero regex edits) and test title-masked retrieval over the combined 9-clause official pool.
- **Equal-Call-Budget Baseline Comparison on a Multi-Threshold Rule-Compiler Stress Test**: On a $16$-seed ($64$-case) synthetic multi-threshold benchmark inspired by healthcare and labor-law rules, we compare `slot_guard_repair` ($26$ calls) against `seed_passthrough` ($0$ calls), `ordinary_style` ($16$ calls), `condition_prompt` ($16$ calls), `structured_json_rewrite` ($16$ calls), and `generic_self_refine` (evaluated at both the matched $26$-call budget and full $32$-call budget), measuring both decision preservation and template novelty ($1 - \kappa_2(q', q_{\text{seed}})$).

## 2. Related Work and Data Provenance Audit

- **Synthetic Queries and Retriever Ranking Sensitivity**: Promptagator (Dai et al., 2023) and InPars-v2 (Jeronymo et al., 2023) use LLM-generated queries to train retrievers, while ARES (Saad-Falcon et al., 2024) and RAGAs (Es et al., 2024) use synthetic queries to evaluate RAG pipelines. Rahmani et al. (SIGIR 2024) and van Elburg et al. (SynDAiTE, 2025) examine macro-level rank correlations and generator biases on synthetic test collections, and Kucia & Gawlik (CIKM 2026 Short / arXiv:2609.14579) show on FiQA that minor query perturbations shift rankings across BM25, dense bi-encoders, and hybrid RRF. Because synthetic-vs.-real ranking shifts are established in prior literature, our Round 1–2 experiments serve as a controlled Chinese insurance baseline that decomposes *source-subsampling variance* from *within-source lexical overlap inflation* ($\kappa_2(q, d^*)$) via paired difference-in-differences ($\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}}$), motivating why retrieval scores alone cannot validate synthetic evaluation sets.
- **Schema-Driven RAG Synthesis and Question Generation Evaluation**: RAGEval (Zhu et al., ACL 2025) extracts domain schemas in finance, medicine, and law to generate RAG benchmarks, DataMorgana (Filice et al., ACL 2025 Industry) provides configurable user/question categorizations for synthetic Q&A generation, and QGEval (Fu et al., EMNLP 2024) evaluates multi-dimensional question generation quality (fluency, clarity, answerability). However, open-loop schema/category prompting (RAGEval, DataMorgana) and surface answerability scoring (QGEval) do not compile source clause thresholds into a formal reachability verifier that partitions generated rewrites into `PRESERVED_DECISION`, `CONDITION_MUTATED` / `COUNTERFACTUAL_FLIP`, and double-witness-certified `DEGRADED_TO_CLARIFY`. Our Section 6 experiments show that single-pass JSON schema prompting and equal-budget unguided LLM self-refinement still leave $25.0\%\text{--}31.2\%$ of multi-threshold rewrites with mutated conditions unless guided by symbolic slot verification.
- **Rule-Following, Clarification Abstention (`CLARIFY`), and Insurance QA**: ShARC (Saeidi et al., EMNLP 2018) formulates rule-grounded QA with follow-up clarification questions, while CLAMBER (Zhang et al., ACL 2024), AskBench (Zhao et al., Findings of ACL 2026), and PRECISE (Divekar et al., ACL 2026 GEM Workshop) evaluate and train downstream assistants to ask clarifying questions in ambiguous dialogues. RC-RAG (Chen et al., Findings of EMNLP 2024) studies rule-contradiction detection in RAG answers, InsuranceQA (Feng et al., 2015/2020) provides community QA pairs, and InsQABench (Ding et al., 2025) benchmarks LLM accuracy on insurance product rules. Our work addresses an orthogonal, upstream problem: rather than testing whether a *downstream answering assistant* asks follow-up questions on given prompts, we audit and repair *the synthetic evaluation queries themselves* during benchmark construction, certifying every ambiguous case via two opposite-outcome completion witnesses and separating non-pivotal slot omissions from true decision ambiguity.

## 3. Round 1: Group-Disjoint Split and Lexical Proxy Diagnostics

### 3.1 Leakage-Free Group Split
Public Chinese insurance FAQ corpora (`baoxianzhidao_filter.csv`, $8{,}362$ raw rows) contain repeated questions and shared boilerplate answers. Filtering for `is_best == '1'`, length bounds ($4 \le |q| \le 500$, $15 \le |d| \le 3{,}000$), and PII removal yields $3{,}746$ unique answers and $3{,}744$ valid QA groups. We build an undirected graph linking any pairs that share normalized questions, normalized answers, or character $2\text{--}4$-gram TF-IDF title cosine similarity $\ge 0.90$, yielding $3{,}725$ connected components via Union-Find. Assigning whole components to splits (`seed=20261025`) produces:
- **Real Calibration Pool**: $750$ queries across $745$ components (zero shared answers with test; max cross-split title cosine $0.8965$).
- **Real Test Set**: $2{,}994$ queries across $2{,}980$ components.

### 3.2 Lexical Systems and Round 1 Results
We index all $3{,}746$ answers under six character $n$-gram lexical retrievers: sublinear TF-IDF cosine similarity over character unigrams (`tfidf_char1`), bigrams (`tfidf_char2`), $1\text{--}3$-grams (`tfidf_char1_3`), and $3\text{--}5$-grams (`tfidf_char3_5`), alongside Okapi BM25 ($k_1=1.2, b=0.75$) over character unigrams (`bm25_char1`) and bigrams (`bm25_char2`).

### 4.2 Decomposing Selection Reversal: Source-Sampling Confounding vs. Lexical Copy Inflation
1. **Full-Test Ordering & Full-Document MaxP Sensitivity Check**: On the $2{,}994$ held-out real questions, `bge_small_zh` achieves $\text{MRR@10}=0.5216$ ($\text{Hit@5}=0.6453$), beating `rrf_bm25_bge` ($0.5030$, $\text{Hit@5}=0.6075$) by $+0.0187$ (10,000-resample paired bootstrap 95% CI: $[+0.0077, +0.0296]$). To rule out 512-token truncation on the $509$ long documents as a confounder, our standalone reproducible `Full-MaxP` sliding-window ablation (`src/maxp_ablation.py`, `results/validation_v2/mitigation_and_maxp_diagnostics.json`, $512$-token windows with $256$-token stride, $762$ overflow tail chunks across $1{,}271$ total windows, $0$ truncated answers; retokenizing each decoded tail string confirms $\le 510$ content tokens and $536/762$ exact token-ID matches) shows `bge_small_zh` still significantly outperforms `rrf_bm25_bge` on `real_test` ($\text{MRR@10}=0.5165$ vs. $0.5007$, $\Delta = +0.0158$, 95% CI $[+0.0049, +0.0267]$; $\text{Hit@5}=0.6389$ vs. $0.6055$).
2. **Disentangling Source-Sampling Confounding from Within-Source Lexical Copy Inflation (`\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}}`)**: Because comparing $N=2{,}994$ real test queries against $N=249$ (`1.5B`) or $N=179$ (`Qwen-Plus`) synthetic queries changes both the target document subset and the query generator, we align real test queries with synthetic queries on the exact same target documents ($207$ matched test documents / $n=209$ real test queries for `1.5B`; $151$ matched test documents / $n=153$ real test queries for `Qwen-Plus`). On these source-matched real subsets, `bm25_char2` happens to be slightly stronger ($0.4073$ and $0.3909$ vs. $0.3817$ full-test) and `bge_small_zh` slightly weaker ($0.4696$ and $0.4668$ vs. $0.5216$ full-test), so `rrf_bm25_bge` already holds a small, statistically non-significant point lead on the matched real queries ($0.4975$ vs. $0.4696$, $\Delta_{\text{BGE}-\text{RRF}} = -0.0279$, 95% CI $[-0.0645, +0.0096]$ for `1.5B`; $0.4980$ vs. $0.4668$, $\Delta_{\text{BGE}-\text{RRF}} = -0.0312$, 95% CI $[-0.0687, +0.0073]$ for `Qwen-Plus`). Thus, **subsampling only $179\text{--}249$ passages introduces substantial document-sampling variance that confounds raw point-winner comparisons against the full $2{,}994$-query deployment distribution**. Holding the $209$ (`1.5B`) and $153$ (`Qwen-Plus`) query-aligned pairs fixed, `zero_shot` synthesis increases mean query-to-source bigram containment $\kappa_2(q, d^*)$ from $0.3253$ to $0.4775$ ($0.4789$ on the $207$ unique docs) on `1.5B` and from $0.3167$ to $0.4638$ ($0.4651$ on $151$ unique docs) on `Qwen-Plus`, significantly inflating `bm25_char2` MRR@10 from $0.4073 \rightarrow 0.4998$ ($0.5036$ on $207$ unique docs; paired gain $+0.0925$, 95% CI $[+0.0227, +0.1633]$) on `1.5B` and from $0.3909 \rightarrow 0.6366$ ($0.6450$ on $151$ unique docs; paired gain $+0.2457$, 95% CI $[+0.1695, +0.3216]$) on `Qwen-Plus`. However, when we test the paired difference-in-differences in RRF's advantage over BGE, $\Delta\Delta_{\text{RRF}-\text{BGE}} = (\text{MRR}_{\text{RRF}} - \text{MRR}_{\text{BGE}})_{\text{syn}} - (\text{MRR}_{\text{RRF}} - \text{MRR}_{\text{BGE}})_{\text{real}}$, all four 10,000-resample paired bootstrap 95% CIs span zero under both query-equal-weighted resampling and source-document-clustered resampling (which averages per unique target document before resampling documents with equal weight): query-weighted $+0.0123$ (CI $[-0.0375, +0.0649]$; document-equal-weighted mean $+0.0185$, clustered CI $[-0.0314, +0.0698]$) on `1.5B zero_shot`, query-weighted $-0.0259$ (CI $[-0.0736, +0.0217]$; doc-weighted mean $-0.0225$, clustered CI $[-0.0710, +0.0258]$) on `1.5B few_shot_3`, query-weighted $-0.0020$ (CI $[-0.0479, +0.0438]$; doc-weighted mean $-0.0036$, clustered CI $[-0.0474, +0.0421]$) on `Qwen-Plus zero_shot`, and query-weighted $-0.0298$ (CI $[-0.0791, +0.0204]$; doc-weighted mean $-0.0320$, clustered CI $[-0.0813, +0.0187]$) on `Qwen-Plus few_shot_3`. 3. **Why Marginal Reweighting Fails vs. Exploratory Relational Source-Overlap Gating (`source_overlap_gated`)**: A 2-fold cross-fit character $1\text{--}3$-gram logistic classifier separates real calibration from synthetic queries with $\text{ROC-AUC}=0.8120$ (`zero_shot`) and $0.7733$ (`few_shot_3`) at $B=25$ (rising to $0.8765$ and $0.9148$ at $B=200$), yet `synthetic_density_weighted` selects `bge_small_zh` in **$0\%$ of trials** across all budgets because the relative advantage between pure dense and hybrid retrieval is governed by the relational query-to-source bigram containment $\kappa_2(q, d^*) = |\text{Bi}(q) \cap \text{Bi}(d^*)| / |\text{Bi}(q)|$. ### 5.3 Key Findings on Condition Drift
1. **Separating Slot Completeness from Reference-Independent Decision Determinacy**: In `src/analyze_v3.py`, we explicitly separate **All-Slot Completeness** (`surface_v2_preserved`, requiring every required slot in $z$ to be recovered) from **Reference-Independent Formal Decision Determinacy** (`enumerate_admissible_decisions` and `evaluate_rule_outcome_under_observed`, which enumerate complete threshold-equivalence regions $\{5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120\}$ across all waiting-period thresholds $\{7, 15, 25, 30, 90\}$ using only extracted question observations and public policy rules—never reading ground-truth `seed_frame` metadata—and check reachability and slot mutations across all six schema fields `ALL_INSURANCE_FIELDS`, including optional waiver slots not in a seed's minimal `required_fields`: returning `PRESERVED_DECISION` iff no schema field is mutated and the reachable set is the singleton $[f(z)]$, `COUNTERFACTUAL_FLIP` / `CONDITION_MUTATED` if any schema field is mutated or the unique reachable decision flips, and `DEGRADED_TO_CLARIFY` when $|\text{reachable}| \neq 1$). For example, when a claim occurs on Day 45 after a 30-day waiting period (`pa_h865`), omitting `cause` (sickness vs. accident) fails all-slot completeness but still yields `WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED` under every admissible completion. Thus, under `ordinary_style` on `Qwen-Max`, standalone all-slot match is $20\%$ ($4/20$) while standalone decision determinacy is $45\%$ ($9/20$); on `Qwen-Plus` `condition_prompt`, all-slot match is $95\%$ ($19/20$, due to one omitted non-pivotal `cause` on a renewal-waiver seed) while decision determinacy is **$100\%$ ($20/20$)**.
2. **Format Validation Does Not Catch Condition Loss**: All $120/120$ generated questions pass permissive `format_valid` ($33/40$ on `1.5B` and $40/40$ on `Qwen-Plus`/`Qwen-Max` under strict single-`?`), yet under `ordinary_style`, $70\%$ (`1.5B`), $100\%$ (`Qwen-Plus`), and $55\%$ (`Qwen-Max`) fail to preserve standalone rule decidability.
3. **Value Mutation on `1.5B` vs. Persistent Conversational Omission Across Tested Models**: On `1.5B`, `ordinary_style` mutates `"保单生效第20天"` into `"保单生效后的第二天"` in $3/20$ seeds (`condition-013, 015, 016`) and collapses `condition-009` (`WAIT_NOT_EXCLUDED`, 7-day wait) and `condition-010` (`WAIT_EXCLUDED`, 25-day wait) into the exact same unanswerable string. Moving to the commercial API models `Qwen-Plus` and `Qwen-Max` (undisclosed parameter counts) reduces observed numeric mutations on this 20-seed sample to $0\%$ ($0/20$; as a finite sample observation, this does not prove zero population error), but both models still omit product names ($100\%$ on `Qwen-Plus`, $50\%$ on `Qwen-Max`) or rewrite first-time enrollment into ambiguous phrasing ($30\%$ on `Qwen-Max`) when instructed to sound colloquial.
4. **Slot-Constrained Prompting (`condition_prompt`) Across Tested Models**: Explicitly constraining required slots restores standalone all-slot completeness to $95\%$ (`1.5B` and `Qwen-Plus`) and $100\%$ (`Qwen-Max`), achieves $95\%\text{--}100\%$ standalone decision determinacy ($100\%$ granted-policy determinacy across all three models), and lifts 5-clause `bm25_char2` Top-1 accuracy to $95\%\text{--}100\%$.

## 7. Practical Recommendations and Limitations

- **Practical Recommendations**:
  1. **Do not equate high synthetic retrieval scores with valid evaluation queries**: Even $100\%$ Top-1 retrieval can mask $>68\%\text{--}93\%$ rates of eligibility-condition omission or threshold mutation under conversational rewriting.
  2. **Compile source rules into a reachability verifier and use symbolic slot repair**: Separate all-slot completeness from reference-independent formal decision determinacy (`PRESERVED_DECISION`, `CONDITION_MUTATED`, and double-witness-certified `DEGRADED_TO_CLARIFY`), and use symbolic verifier feedback (`slot_guard_repair`) rather than unguided LLM self-refinement.
  3. **Control for source-sampling variance, audit $\kappa_2(q', d^*)$, and apply small-sample candidate-set abstention**: Pair synthetic benchmarks with source-matched real diagnostics, inspect sensitivity across overlap quantiles ($q \in [0.10, 0.30]$), and use paired-bootstrap candidate-set abstention on $25\text{--}50$ group-disjoint real calibration queries.
- **Limitations**: First, our Round 1–2 FAQ corpus (`baoxianzhidao_filter.csv`, via `InsuranceModel`/`ChineseNlpCorpus`) originates from historical Baidu Zhidao community Q&A with weak best-answer labels, unverified open redistribution licensing, and potential foundation-model pretraining exposure, and our $249$/$179$ synthetic sets exhibit source-subsampling variance relative to the full $2{,}994$-query test set; moreover, `source_overlap_gated` was developed retrospectively and is sensitive to the cutoff quantile ($q \ge 0.40\text{--}0.50$ reverts to RRF). Second, our generators belong to the Qwen family (`Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, `Qwen-Plus`, and `Qwen-Max`) evaluated strictly in inference mode without weight fine-tuning. Third, while our nine official insurance clauses ($5$ dev + $4$ held-out) are anchored to SHA256-verified insurer PDFs (with out-of-sample confirmation showing $15/16$ automatic and $16/16$ human-verified decision preservation under the frozen Round 3 parser), our Section 6 benchmark uses researcher-constructed synthetic rule texts rather than verbatim statutes, and independent two-rater human annotation (`reviewer_A.html` and `reviewer_B.html`, scored via `score_annotations.py`) remains pending.
